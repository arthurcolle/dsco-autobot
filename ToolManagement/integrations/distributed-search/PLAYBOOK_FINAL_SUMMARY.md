## 🎯 KEY INSIGHTS BY PART

**Part 0:** Most startups fail due to founder burnout, not bad ideas. Mental health, relationships, and financial runway matter more than you think.

**Part 1:** Know your customer archetypes deeply. Solo developers need different messaging than enterprises. Tailor everything.

**Part 2:** Build the minimum viable product in 4 weeks. Don't spend 6 months building in a vacuum. Ship and iterate.

**Part 3:** First 10 customers require manual hustle. Friends & family → HN/Reddit → Product Hunt → Direct outreach. No shortcuts.

**Part 4:** Getting from $1K to $10K MRR takes 12-18 months at 20% MoM growth. Pick 2-3 channels, build systems, optimize pricing, hire strategically, maintain rhythm.

**Part 5:** Track North Star metric (value delivered), cohort retention (predict future), unit economics (LTV:CAC >3:1), and retention curves (aim for smiling curve).

**Part 6:** The first dollar changes everything psychologically, but nothing practically. It's validation, not victory. Celebrate, then get back to work.

**Part 7:** Building is 80% psychology. Slow growth feels bad but is normal. Comparison is the thief of joy. The trough (months 4-9) is where most quit. Find your tribe.

**Part 8:** Avoid feature factory, premature scaling, ignoring customers, building in vacuum, chasing shiny objects, and optimizing too early. Focus is your superpower.

**Part 9:** Consistent weekly rhythm beats heroic sprints. Monday plan, Tue-Thu execute, Friday review, Weekend rest. Compound small wins.

**Part 10:** Use checklists and templates to reduce decision fatigue. Copy-paste and customize for your business.
