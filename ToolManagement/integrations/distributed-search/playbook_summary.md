## KEY THEMES THROUGHOUT:

1. **Do Things That Don't Scale**
   - Manual outreach early
   - Personal onboarding
   - Hand-holding customers
   - Then systematize

2. **Consistency Over Intensity**
   - 20% MoM growth for 14 months
   - Better than 40% for 3 months then burnout
   - Sustainable > heroic

3. **Measure What Matters**
   - MRR, churn, CAC, LTV
   - Not vanity metrics
   - Weekly reviews
   - Data-driven decisions

4. **Build Relationships**
   - Not transactions
   - Community engagement
   - Customer success
   - Long-term thinking

5. **Mental Health First**
   - Therapy, exercise, sleep
   - Boundaries with work
   - Protect relationships
   - Sustainable pace

6. **Systems Enable Scale**
   - Automate the repeatable
   - Keep relationships manual
   - Build playbooks
   - Document processes

## HOW TO USE THIS PLAYBOOK:

**If you're at $0 MRR:**
- Start with Part 0 (get your head right)
- Then Part 1 (understand customers)
- Then Part 2 (build MVP)
- Then Part 3 (get first 10 customers)

**If you're at $1K MRR:**
- Review Part 4.1 (understand the math)
- Pick 2-3 channels from Part 4.2
- Implement systems from Part 4.3
- Track metrics religiously

**If you're at $5K MRR:**
- Double down on working channels
- Consider paid ads (if unit economics work)
- Start building team (Part 4.5)
- Optimize pricing (Part 4.4)

**If you're stuck:**
- Review Part 0.8 (failure reality)
- Check Part 8 (anti-patterns)
- Talk to customers
- Reassess product-market fit
