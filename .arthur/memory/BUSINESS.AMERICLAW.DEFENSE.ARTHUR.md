**AmeriClaw** — American-focused agentic CLI (Rust, built on Anthropic Claude). Positioning against Chinese AI supply chain risk and OpenAI-acquired OpenClaw.

**Target market:** US defense contractors, military-adjacent enterprises.

**Technical differentiators:**
- Rust = NSA-recommended memory-safe language (2022 memo)
- Cargo.lock = SBOM (CMMC compliance)
- Local-first / air-gappable (Ollama provider)
- XMPP adapter (DISA military messaging standard)
- No Chinese-origin dependencies (verifiable)

**Channel strategy (American-safe only):**
- IN: XMPP, Mattermost, Matrix, MS Teams, Email/SMTP, Signal, NATS
- OUT: Telegram (Russian), WhatsApp (unacceptable for CUI/ITAR)

**Path to first revenue:**
1. SAM.gov registration (free)
2. SBIR Phase I application ($50-250k if awarded)
3. Subcontract to a prime (Lockheed, Raytheon, SAIC, Booz Allen)

**Compliance:** CMMC 2.0, NIST 800-171, FIPS 140-2/3, FAR/DFARS, ITAR/EAR, MIL-STD-882E

**Financial context:** ~$4k runway. Bootstrapped. Needs to move fast.
